How a user moves around a MeoUI app. Window chrome, the overflow menu, Settings and About are in APP_SHELL.md; the sidebar route contract is in SETTINGS.md. This page covers the choices around them.

1. Choose the navigation surface

SituationComponent
2–5 top-level destinations on a phone-width windowMeoNavigationBar (bottom bar)
Same destinations, medium windowMeoNavigationRail
Same destinations, wide windowMeoSidebar (persistent, search, groups)
More than 5 destinationsSidebar groups; bottom bar keeps the first few and an overflow that calls openSidebar()
Sections inside one destinationMeoTabs
A filter or sort of the same listMeoFilterGroup / chips, MeoSegmentedButtons
A hierarchy deeper than two levelsMeoBreadcrumbs on wide windows, back arrow on compact
Steps of one taskMeoStepper

MeoAppLayout and MeoNavigationSuite choose the right one of the first three from the window class; use them instead of switching by hand.

2. Destinations

  • Few and stable. Five or fewer primary destinations. Each is a place, not an action: Home, Explore, Installed, Updates are destinations; "Install" is not.
  • Same order everywhere. The bottom bar, rail and sidebar show the same items in the same order; the sidebar may add groups.
  • Stable route ids. Each destination has a unique route string. Selection is by route identity, never by row index, so filtering or reordering cannot select the wrong page.
  • Icon + label. Bottom bar labelType: "always" unless space forces "selected". A rail without labels needs tooltips and accessible names.
  • Badges (badgeText, badgeDot) mean "needs attention" (Updates: 3). A count is real or absent.
  • Settings and About are not primary destinations (APP_SHELL.md).
  • Search is a toolbar capability (MeoSearchBar, MeoSearchView), not a permanent destination, unless search is the app's whole purpose.

3. Top app bar

TypeUse
smallDefault for pages and list/detail
centerA single-destination utility or a dialog-like page
medium / largeLanding pages that benefit from a headline; collapse on scroll
contextual (isContextual, selectionCount)While items are selected: shows count and bulk actions, replaces the normal bar

Rules: ≤3 trailing actions, the rest in the overflow (more_vert, last); the leading control is exactly one of sidebar toggle, menu, or arrow_back (only with a real parent).

4. Moving between pages

  • A top-level switch replaces the content without a back stack.
  • Opening an item pushes a route and shows arrow_back. Back returns to the same scroll position and selection (preserveScrollPosition).
  • MeoPageHost animates by direction (1 forward, −1 back) with a 32dp travel; Reduce Motion makes the change instant.
  • Deep content never needs more than two levels under a destination (PAGE_TYPES.md); a third level is a transient overlay (task sheet, dialog), not another page.
  • Keyboard: Ctrl+, opens Settings, Ctrl+? shortcuts and F1 help (APP_SHELL.md). Recommended, not yet enforced by a component: Escape or Alt+Left goes back where a back arrow is shown, and Ctrl+F focuses search where search exists.

5. Tabs, segmented buttons and chips

ControlSwitchesRemember on return
MeoTabsParallel sections of one page (Overview, Reviews, Changes)Recommended: restore the selected tab
MeoSegmentedButtons2–5 mutually exclusive views or modes of the same data (List/Grid)Optional
MeoFilterChip / MeoFilterGroupFilters that narrow the same listOptional

Do not use tabs for navigation between unrelated areas. Do not use more than six tabs; use isScrollable only when localized labels need it.

6. Search

  • MeoSearchBar sits in the top bar (wide) or opens a MeoSearchView (compact).
  • Show recent and suggested queries (MeoSearchSuggestions) before typing.
  • Results update while typing with a debounce; show MeoLoadingFeedback only after the 120 ms delay, and an empty state for no results (STATES.md).
  • Search the destination list (searchResults) or the content, and say which in the placeholder ("Search navigation" vs "Search apps").

7. Checklist

  • ☐ ≤5 primary destinations, same order on every size.
  • ☐ Routes are unique stable strings; selection by route.
  • ☐ Back returns to the previous scroll position.
  • ☐ Contextual bar appears for multi-select and restores on exit.
  • ☐ Keyboard and Escape behavior documented in the page.
  • ☐ Every icon-only destination has a tooltip and accessible name.
